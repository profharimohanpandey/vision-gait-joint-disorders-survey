# Corrections and contributions

Please include the citation key or figure number when reporting a problem. For a citation correction, give the affected field and a publisher, DOI or indexing record that supports the change. For a figure, identify the PDF or PNG and the label or detail that needs attention. [Open an issue](https://github.com/profharimohanpandey/vision-gait-joint-disorders-survey/issues)

When suggesting a study, tell us which joint, measurement or method it addresses. A short note about its population, recording conditions and outcome helps us place it in the review. [Review method and scope](METHODOLOGY.md)

Please keep citation keys stable when correcting metadata. Update the BibTeX, CSV and JSON records together so the complete bibliography and the category index continue to agree. Record the basis for a verified change in the metadata log. [Reference data](references/README.md)

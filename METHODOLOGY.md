# Review method and scope

The paper takes a structured narrative approach. It starts with a clinical question about hip, knee or ankle mobility, identifies the gait variable relevant to that question, and considers how video can estimate it and which reference can support the estimate.

| Review question | Where to read |
| --- | --- |
| Which gait changes are associated with hip, knee and ankle problems, including compensation at adjacent joints? | §§3 and 5, and S1–S3 |
| How do vision methods use body landmarks, gait features and action labels to measure or recognise movement? | §§4 and 6, and S4–S6 |
| What references, clinical labels and evaluation designs support claims about motion, severity or change? | §§5, 6.5 and 7 |
| How do recording conditions and the study population affect the interpretation of video estimates? | §7 and the supporting comparisons |

## Search concepts

The search covered IEEE Xplore, the ACM Digital Library, Springer and ScienceDirect. The reported search window considered English-language peer-reviewed journal and conference papers published through April 2026.

| Concept | Examples of recorded terms |
| --- | --- |
| Movement | Gait analysis |
| Joint and clinical setting | Joint mobility, hip, knee, ankle, rehabilitation |
| Vision and analysis method | Computer vision, markerless gait analysis, human pose estimation, human action recognition, deep learning |

The paper records the sources and concept groups. The exact query strings and field settings for individual databases were unavailable.

## Selection counts and available records

Duplicates were filtered before title and abstract screening. Full texts were then assessed for scope, outcomes, evaluation, publication eligibility and availability.

| Criterion | Include | Exclude |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scope | Clinical work on hip, knee or ankle mobility, or an evaluated method relevant to lower-limb gait or motion | Unrelated tasks and populations, including identity recognition without an applicable joint-assessment method |
| Evidence | A relevant gait, biomechanical or functional outcome, or a relevant technical evaluation | Reports without an applicable outcome or evaluation |
| Publication | An English-language peer-reviewed journal or conference paper through April 2026 | Reports outside the stated publication, language or date criteria |
| Access and duplication | An available full text and one record per report | Unavailable full text and duplicate records |

## Interpreting evidence

Read a result in light of the gait variable, the participant group, the camera arrangement and the reference measurement. Agreement with motion capture supports an angle estimate under the tested conditions. Clinical inference needs a relevant population, an appropriate clinical outcome and a design that can test the claim.
